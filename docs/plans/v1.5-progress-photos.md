# Implementation Plan: `v1.5` — Progress Photos & Timeline Scrubber *(Low-Priority Backlog)*

> **Agent Prompt**: Implement `v1.5` according to this plan. Prerequisite: `v0.10` is complete. Read `docs/01-architecture-and-tech-stack.md` and `docs/02-database-and-sync-spec.md`.

## 1. Goal & Scope

Build the privacy-first **Progress Photos & Timeline Scrubber** module, stripping all EXIF and GPS location metadata on-device before storage, saving photos locally in OPFS/IndexedDB (and syncing privately to Cloudflare R2 when signed in), and providing a **Video-Style Timeline Scrubber** and **Before / After Split Comparison Slider** linked to your **Trend Weight** on each photo's date.

- **In Scope**: Client-side Canvas/WebP compression + EXIF/GPS stripper, local blob cache + authenticated Cloudflare R2 upload/download (`/api/photos/:id`), Pose categories (`Front`, `Back`, `Side`), interactive Timeline Scrubber playback, Before/After split comparison slider with date & Trend Weight overlays.
- **Out of Scope**: Public photo sharing links (all progress photos are strictly private to the user).

## 3. Step-by-Step Implementation Tasks

### Step 1: On-Device EXIF/GPS Stripper & Private R2 Storage (Linear 15.2)
1. Implement `stripExifAndCompressImage(file, maxDimension = 1600, quality = 0.85)` in `apps/web/src/utils/image-privacy.ts`:
   - Draws the source image onto an offscreen `<canvas>` (respecting orientation) and exports a fresh `image/webp` `Blob`. Re-encoding via canvas guarantees **100% of EXIF, camera serial numbers, and GPS coordinates are stripped on the device** before the bytes ever leave the browser.
2. Store the compressed WebP blob locally (for instant offline viewing and Demo Mode) and, when authenticated, upload to `PUT /api/photos/:id` (stored in Cloudflare R2 under `users/${userId}/photos/${photoId}.webp`, gated by Better Auth session/token middleware).
3. Record metadata in SQLite `progress_photos` (`id`, `userId`, `date`, `pose`, `scaleWeightLb`, `trendWeightLb`, `bodyFatPct`, `notes`, `r2Key`).

### Step 2: Video-Style Timeline Scrubber & Before/After Split Slider (Linear 15.2)
1. Build `ProgressTimelineScrubber`:
   - Filters by `pose` (`Front`, `Back`, `Side`) so body position stays consistent across frames.
   - Includes a **Play / Pause** button (selectable playback speed: `1x`, `2x`, `4x`) and a horizontal **Timeline Scrubber Slider** that smoothly flips through every photo from oldest to newest while displaying the live **Date, Scale Weight, and Trend Weight** badge in the corner.
2. Build `BeforeAfterSlider`:
   - Pick any two photos (`Before Date` vs. `After Date`).
   - Drag a vertical divider handle left and right across the overlaid images, showing the exact days elapsed and $\Delta\text{Trend Weight}$ (`-11.4 lb over 12 weeks`).
