# Implementation Plan: `v0.8` — Nutrition AI (Gemini Flash-Lite Vision, Label OCR & Text)

> **Agent Prompt**: Implement `v0.8` according to this plan. Prerequisite: `v0.7` is complete. Read `docs/04-nutrition-and-tdee-spec.md` and `docs/05-ai-coach-and-mcp-spec.md` for reference.

---

## 1. Goal & Scope

Add optional, BYOK-powered **Multimodal Nutrition AI** using **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite** (free tier by default, plus optional OpenAI, Anthropic, or local Ollama keys) to estimate calories and protein from **Food Photos**, scan **Nutrition Facts Labels via Camera OCR**, and parse **Natural Language Meal Descriptions**.

- **In Scope**: BYOK AI Settings & master AI off-switch, Vercel AI SDK (`ai`) structured output service, Photo-to-Meal estimator with portion/gram adjustment sliders, Nutrition Facts Label Camera OCR (fallback when barcode is missing), Natural Language text food logger.
- **Out of Scope**: AI Workout Coach & MCP Server (`v0.9`).

## 3. Step-by-Step Implementation Tasks

### Step 1: Zod AI Schemas & Multi-Provider BYOK Client (Linear 8.1)
1. In `packages/shared/src/ai-nutrition-schemas.ts`, define strict `zod` schemas for `generateObject()`:
   - `PhotoMealEstimationSchema`:
     - `mealTitle`: string
     - `items`: array of `{ name, estimatedWeightGrams, caloriesPer100g, proteinPer100g, calories, proteinG, confidence: number (0..1), reasoningNote }`
   - `NutritionLabelOcrSchema`:
     - `{ productName?, servingSizeGrams, caloriesPerServing, proteinGramsPerServing, carbsGramsPerServing?, fatGramsPerServing? }`
   - `NaturalLanguageMealSchema`:
     - `items`: array of `{ name, weightGrams, calories, proteinG }`
2. In `apps/web/src/services/ai-provider.ts` & `ai-settings-card.tsx`:
   - Install `ai`, `@ai-sdk/google`, `@ai-sdk/openai`, `@ai-sdk/anthropic`.
   - Default model: `google('gemini-2.5-flash-lite')` (using the user's Google AI Studio free-tier API key stored in local settings).
   - Also support OpenAI (`gpt-4o-mini`), Anthropic (`claude-3-5-haiku`), or a custom OpenAI-compatible base URL (e.g., `http://localhost:11434/v1` for local Ollama).
   - When the **"Enable AI Features"** master switch in Settings is `false`, all AI buttons on the Nutrition and Workout screens are completely hidden.

### Step 2: AI Photo-to-Meal Estimator with Gram Sliders (Linear 8.2)
1. Build `AiPhotoMealModal`:
   - Users snap or choose a photo of their meal (client-side resized to max `1024px` WebP so inference takes `<2s` and uses minimal tokens).
   - Optional text hint field (e.g., *"I weighed the steak at 220g raw, cooked in 10g butter"* — if user provides exact gram weights in the note, the prompt instructs Gemini Flash-Lite to lock those exact gram weights!).
   - Displays each detected food item with an interactive **Gram Weight Slider / Input** that dynamically recalculates `calories` and `proteinG` from `caloriesPer100g` and `proteinPer100g` before clicking **"Log Meal"**.

### Step 3: Nutrition Facts Label OCR & Natural Language Logger (Linear 8.3)
1. Build `AiLabelOcrModal`:
   - Accessible directly from the Barcode Scanner (*"Barcode not found? Scan Nutrition Label"*) or the Nutrition toolbar.
   - Snaps a photo of any package's **Nutrition Facts table**.
   - Gemini Flash-Lite extracts `servingSizeGrams`, `caloriesPerServing`, and `proteinGramsPerServing`, normalizes to per-100g values, saves the item to local SQLite `food_items`, and opens the gram-weight logger.
2. Build `AiTextFoodBar`:
   - Quick natural-language input bar at the top of the Nutrition modal: type *"3 scrambled eggs, 2 slices Dave's Killer Bread, and 200g cottage cheese"* $\rightarrow$ previews itemized rows with gram weights, calories, and protein $\rightarrow$ 1-click confirm.

## 5. Manual Verification Checklist (User Sign-Off)

1. Go to **Settings $\rightarrow$ AI Configuration**:
   - Toggle **"Enable AI Features"** off and verify all AI buttons disappear from the Nutrition screen.
   - Toggle it back on, enter a free-tier **Gemini API Key**, and click **"Test Connection"** to verify a green success badge.
2. Open **Nutrition $\rightarrow$ AI Photo Estimate**:
   - Upload a photo of a meal (with an optional note like *"200g chicken breast + rice"*).
   - Verify Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite returns an itemized breakdown and that dragging the gram weight slider updates calories and protein smoothly before saving.
3. Open **Scan Nutrition Label (OCR)**:
   - Upload/snap a photo of a Nutrition Facts label and verify the exact serving weight (grams), calories, and protein are extracted and saved to your food library.
4. Type *"250g Greek yogurt with 30g whey protein and 100g blueberries"* into the **Natural Language Food Bar** and verify it parses into 3 distinct items with accurate calories and protein.
