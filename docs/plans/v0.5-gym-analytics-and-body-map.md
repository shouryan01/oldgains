## 1. Goal & Scope

Build the complete **Analytics & History** suite featuring the **3-Mode Anatomical Body Map** (Weekly Fractional Effective Sets vs. MEV/MAV/MRV hypertrophy landmarks, Muscle Fatigue/Recovery decay, and Untrained muscles), iOS-style gradient **Estimated 1RM Progression Charts**, **Structural Balance Ratios**, a **52-Week Activity Heatmap**, and full post-workout editing/backdating of completed workouts.

- **In Scope**: Fractional muscle volume engine (`packages/shared`), 48–72h exponential muscle fatigue decay engine, Structural Balance ratio calculator, Recharts gradient charts, 52-week SVG activity heatmap, Exercise Detail 1RM chart & history tab, editable past workouts & backdated paper workout logger.
- **Out of Scope**: Body weight EWMA & CSV import (`v0.6`), Nutrition charts (`v0.7`).

## 3. Step-by-Step Implementation Tasks

### Step 1: Muscle Analytics & Structural Balance Engines (`packages/shared`) (Linear 5.1 & 5.2)
1. In `packages/shared/src/muscle-analytics.ts`:
   - **Mode 1 — Weekly Fractional Effective Sets (`calculateWeeklyMuscleVolume`)**:
     - For every completed working set (where `rir === null || rir <= 4`) in the selected 7-day window, add `1.0 * muscleMap.weights[muscle]` to each of the 17 `MuscleMapKey`s (also compute total reps and total tonnage `reps * weightLb * muscleMap.weights[muscle]`).
     - Classify each muscle against science-based hypertrophy landmarks:
       - `under_mev` (`< 6` fractional sets)
       - `optimal_mav` (`6–18` fractional sets)
       - `approaching_mrv` (`> 18` fractional sets)
   - **Mode 2 — Muscle Fatigue & Recovery State (`calculateMuscleFatigue`)**:
     - For each completed workout in the past 96 hours, compute initial fatigue impulse per muscle from fractional sets weighted by effort factor (`1.2` if `RIR 0`, `1.0` if `RIR 1–2`, `0.75` if `RIR 3+`).
     - Apply exponential decay $F(t) = F_0 \cdot e^{-\Delta t / \tau}$ ($\tau = 36\text{h}$ for small muscles like biceps/delts/calves, $\tau = 54\text{h}$ for chest/back/quads/hamstrings) and clamp to `0–100%` fatigue (`Fresh < 25%`, `Recovering 25–65%`, `Fatigued > 65%`).
   - **Mode 3 — Untrained Muscles (`findUntrainedMuscles`)**:
     - Identifies muscles with `< 1.0` fractional sets over the past 14 days.
2. In `packages/shared/src/structural-balance.ts`:
   - Compute Push vs. Pull volume ratio (Horizontal Push : Horizontal Pull, Vertical Push : Vertical Pull, Anterior Chain : Posterior Chain).
   - Compute 1RM strength ratios against classic benchmarks (e.g., Overhead Press : Bench Press : Squat : Deadlift = `0.6 : 1.0 : 1.35 : 1.65`) and Lifts as a `% of Body Weight`.

### Step 2: 3-Mode Muscle Map, Gradient Charts & Year Heatmap (`apps/web`) (Linear 5.1 & 5.2)
1. Build `MuscleMapDashboard`:
   - Segmented control switching between **Volume (MEV/MAV/MRV)**, **Recovery / Fatigue**, and **Untrained**, plus sub-metric pills for **Effective Sets**, **Total Reps**, and **Tonnage (`Reps × Weight`)**.
   - Renders `AnatomicalBodyMap` alongside `HypertrophyLandmarksChart` (horizontal bars with shaded optimal `6–18 set` green band).
2. Build `OneRmProgressionChart` using Recharts `<AreaChart>` with iOS-style `<linearGradient>` fill, time-range selector (`1M`, `3M`, `6M`, `1Y`, `All`), and embed it in both the `Analytics` page and `ExerciseDetailSheet`.
3. Build `YearActivityHeatmap` rendering a 52-week $\times$ 7-day SVG grid with hover tooltips (`Date`, `Workout Name`, `Duration`, `Volume`, `PRs`) and Lifetime Total Weight Lifted counter.

### Step 3: Editable Workout History & Backdated Paper Workout Logger (Linear 5.3)
1. Build `WorkoutHistoryEditor`:
   - Chronological list of completed workouts broken down by month/week with mini Muscle Maps, duration, volume, and PR pills.
   - Clicking any past workout allows editing sets, adding/removing exercises, changing the workout date/time, or deleting the session.
   - Add a **"Log Past / Paper Workout"** button to enter a completed session on any historical calendar date.
   - After any historical edit or deletion, run `recomputePersonalRecordsForExercises(db, affectedExerciseIds)` so all PR badges and 1RM charts reflect the corrected history.

## 5. Manual Verification Checklist (User Sign-Off)

1. Open the **Analytics** tab (with Demo Data seeded or after completing a workout in `v0.4`).
2. Test all 3 modes of the **Anatomical Body Map**:
   - **Volume Landmarks**: Verify fractional sets per muscle (`1.0` primary, `0.4` secondary) are plotted against the `6–18 set` optimal hypertrophy zone, and toggle between `Sets`, `Reps`, and `Weight × Reps`.
   - **Fatigue / Recovery**: Verify muscles trained today appear as **Fatigued (Red/Amber)** with estimated hours until full recovery.
   - **Untrained**: Verify muscles not hit in the last 14 days are highlighted.
3. Inspect the **Estimated 1RM Progression Chart**, **Structural Balance Radar/Ratios**, and **52-Week Activity Heatmap**.
4. Open the **History** section, edit a weight in a past completed workout (or log a backdated workout from last week), and verify that the 1RM curve, heatmap, and PR records immediately recalculate.
