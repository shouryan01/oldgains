# Implementation Plan: `v1.3` — Fitness Wrapped (Ported to Web & iOS)

> **Agent Prompt**: Implement `v1.3` according to this plan. Prerequisite: `v0.10` (for Web) and `v1.1` (for iOS) are complete. Inspect the existing `ios/fitness-wrapped` project (if present in the workspace) and `docs/06-ios-watch-and-wrapped-spec.md`.

---

## 1. Goal & Scope

Port and expand **`ios/fitness-wrapped`** into a first-class, interactive **Fitness Wrapped** story experience across **both Web and iOS**, computing rich training and nutrition insights for any selected year or time window (`Full Year`, `Last 90 Days`, `Last 30 Days`, `All Time`) and exporting high-resolution 9:16 shareable cards.

- **In Scope**: Shared Fitness Wrapped calculation engine (`packages/shared/src/wrapped.ts` + Swift `WrappedEngine.swift`), 8 interactive story slides (Total Tonnage & fun weight equivalents, Consistency & Peak Hours, Top Exercises, Muscle Archetype & Body Map, PR Hall of Fame, Strength Progression, Nutrition & Protein Totals, Final Summary Card), full-screen tap/swipe Story Player on Web (`motion/react`) and iOS (`SwiftUI`), PNG share card export.
- **Out of Scope**: External social network auto-posting (uses native Web Share API / iOS `ShareLink` + PNG download).

## 3. Step-by-Step Implementation Tasks

### Step 1: Shared Fitness Wrapped Aggregation Engine (`packages/shared` & Swift) (Linear 14.1)
1. Implement `generateFitnessWrapped(db, dateRange)` in `packages/shared/src/wrapped-engine.ts` (and `WrappedEngine.swift` on iOS) producing the 8 story slide models:
   - **Slide 1 — Consistency & Time Invested**: Total workouts completed, total hours spent training, longest weekly streak, favourite training weekday, and chronotype badge (*"5 AM Club"*, *"Lunch Break Lifter"*, *"Night Owl"*).
   - **Slide 2 — Total Tonnage Lifted**: Cumulative `weight × reps` across all sets, paired with fun real-world comparisons (e.g., *Boeing 747s (`400,000 lb`)*, *African Elephants (`13,000 lb`)*, *Toyota Camrys (`3,500 lb`)*, *Statue of Liberty*).
   - **Slide 3 — Top Exercises & Rep Milestones**: Top 5 most-performed exercises by set count and total volume, plus total lifetime reps completed.
   - **Slide 4 — Anatomical Muscle Archetype**: Full-period 17-region `AnatomicalBodyMap` heatmap and assigned training persona (e.g., *"Balanced Hypertrophy Architect"*, *"Upper Body Specialist"*, *"Posterior Chain Powerhouse"*).
   - **Slide 5 — PR Hall of Fame**: Biggest 1RM and weight jumps achieved during the period (`Start Weight -> Peak Weight (+% gain)`).
   - **Slide 6 — Nutrition & Fuel Highlights** (shown if nutrition/weight logged): Total grams of protein consumed (with fun equivalent), most-logged food/saved meal, and **Trend Weight** transformation.
   - **Slide 7 — Structural Balance & Volume Score**: Overall symmetry score (`0–100`) and strongest lift as a `% of Body Weight`.
   - **Slide 8 — Shareable Wrapped Summary Card**: Compact Bento-grid summary combining all headline stats, mini Muscle Map, and year/period badge.

### Step 2: Interactive Web & iOS Story Player + 9:16 Card Export (Linear 14.2)
1. Build `FitnessWrappedPlayer` on Web (`motion/react`) and `FitnessWrappedStoryView` on iOS (`SwiftUI`):
   - Instagram/Spotify-Wrapped style segmented progress bars at the top of the screen (auto-advances every 7 seconds, hold finger/mouse to pause, tap left/right edges to navigate).
   - Rich animated mesh gradients and spring number counters.
2. Add **"Share / Download Card"** on Slide 8:
   - On Web: renders the 1080×1920 Bento summary card to a high-DPI `<canvas>` / PNG blob and triggers `navigator.share()` or PNG download.
   - On iOS: uses SwiftUI `ImageRenderer` + `ShareLink` to save to Photos or share to Messages/Instagram.

## 5. Manual Verification Checklist (User Sign-Off)

1. Go to **Analytics $\rightarrow$ Fitness Wrapped** on the Web app (and iOS app if built) and select **Year in Review** or **All Time**.
2. Tap through all **8 animated story slides** and verify:
   - Total tonnage and fun real-world equivalents calculate accurately from your logged sets.
   - The **Muscle Archetype** slide highlights your most-trained muscles on the SVG body map.
   - Your top exercises, PR breakthroughs, and protein/weight stats match your history.
3. On the final slide, click **"Download / Share Card"** and verify a crisp 9:16 PNG summary card downloads cleanly.
