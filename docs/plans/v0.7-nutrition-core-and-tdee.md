## 1. Goal & Scope

Build the **Calories & Protein-First Nutrition Tracker**, featuring gram-weight food scaling, Custom Foods & Saved Meals ("Copy Yesterday", "Explode Meal"), a browser camera **Barcode Scanner** integrated with **Open Food Facts**, and the MacroFactor-inspired **Dynamic Expenditure (TDEE) Engine** with **Partial-Day Logging Guardrails** and **Weekly Strategy Check-Ins**.

- **In Scope**: Calories & Protein dual-ring dashboard, gram-weight scaling, Saved Meals & Quick Add, Camera Barcode Scanner (`BarcodeDetector` + `zxing-wasm` fallback) + Open Food Facts API client + local SQLite food cache, Rolling 14-Day Dynamic TDEE engine, Partial-Day logging toggle & `<50%` auto-detection, Diet Goal phases (Cut / Lean Bulk / Maintenance) with Lean Body Mass protein scaling, Weekly Strategy Check-In card.
- **Out of Scope**: Gemini Flash-Lite Photo/OCR/Text AI logging (`v0.8`).

## 3. Step-by-Step Implementation Tasks

### Step 1: Dynamic TDEE Engine, Partial-Day Guardrail & Weekly Check-In Math (`packages/shared`) (Linear 7.3)
1. In `packages/shared/src/tdee-engine.ts`, implement `calculateDynamicExpenditure(nutritionDays, trendWeights, dietGoal)`:
   - **Partial-Day Filter**: A day is excluded from calorie averaging if `day.isPartial === true` OR (`day.totalCalories > 0` and `day.totalCalories < 0.5 * day.targetCalories`). Also expose `isSuspectedPartialDay(day)` so the UI can prompt the user.
   - **Rolling 14-Day Expenditure**:
     - Require at least 7 complete nutrition days and 4 weigh-ins in the 14-day window (computing a `confidenceScore` from `0` to `100%` based on data density).
     - Compute daily tissue energy change from Trend Weight:
       $$\Delta E_{\text{tissue}} = \frac{\text{Trend}_t - \text{Trend}_{t-14}}{14} \times 3500\text{ kcal/lb}$$
     - Compute raw expenditure:
       $$\text{TDEE}_{\text{raw}} = \overline{\text{Calories}}_{\text{14d, complete}} - \Delta E_{\text{tissue}}$$
     - Smooth $\text{TDEE}_{\text{raw}}$ with EWMA ($\beta = 0.2$) to produce `estimatedTdeeKcal`.
   - **Target Calories & Protein Calculation (`computeCheckInRecommendation`)**:
     - Target Calories = $\text{estimatedTdeeKcal} + \left(\frac{\text{weeklyRateTargetLb} \times 3500}{7}\right)$.
     - Target Protein (g): If `bodyFatPct` is logged, $1.1\text{g} \times \text{LeanBodyMassLb}$; otherwise $0.9\text{g} \times \text{TrendWeightLb}$.
2. Write unit tests in `tdee-engine.test.ts` covering cutting, bulking, maintenance, and partial-day exclusions.

### Step 2: Calories & Protein Dashboard, Gram Scaling & Saved Meals (`apps/web`) (Linear 7.1)
1. Build `NutritionDashboard`:
   - Prominent twin gradient progress rings: **Calories (Consumed / Target / Remaining)** and **Protein (g / Target)**. (Carbs and Fat are tucked away unless enabled via the `"Show Secondary Macros"` toggle in Settings).
   - **Partial-Day Guardrail Banner**: Toggle switch `"Mark Day Incomplete"` (or automatic warning banner when yesterday's calories were `<50%` of target) so forgotten meals never distort TDEE.
2. Build `FoodLoggerModal` & `SavedMealsDrawer`:
   - **Gram-Weight Scaling**: Every food item stores `caloriesPer100g` and `proteinPer100g` (plus default `servingGrams`). Typing `185g` on your food scale updates calories and protein live as you type.
   - **Saved Meals**: Save any group of items as a reusable Meal/Recipe. When logging a Saved Meal, provide two buttons: **Log as Single Item** or **Explode into Ingredients** (inserts each ingredient separately so you can adjust today's gram weights).
   - **1-Tap Copy Yesterday**: Copy yesterday's Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snack in 1 click.
   - **Quick Add**: Log raw `Calories` + `Protein (g)` + optional label in 2 seconds.

### Step 3: Browser Camera Barcode Scanner & Open Food Facts Lookup (Linear 7.2)
1. Build `BarcodeScannerModal`:
   - Opens device camera via `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia({ video: { facingMode: 'environment' } })`.
   - Uses native `window.BarcodeDetector` when supported (Chrome/Android/macOS), falling back to `@sec-ant/zxing-wasm` (or manual barcode number entry) on browsers without `BarcodeDetector`.
   - On scan, checks local SQLite `food_items` by `barcode` first; if not cached, fetches `https://world.openfoodfacts.org/api/v2/product/{barcode}.json`, caches the product in `food_items`, and opens the gram-weight input ready to log.
